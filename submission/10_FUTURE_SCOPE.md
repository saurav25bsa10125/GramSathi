# 10. Future Scope & Commercial Roadmap — GramSathi

---

## 1. Hardware Integration Roadmap (Phase 2)
- **LoRaWAN Livestock Collars:** Deploy ESP32-based collar hardware with MAX30102 pulse oximeter/temperature sensor and sub-GHz long-range LoRa mesh to cover 5 km village pasture radius.
- **Solar Ultrasonic Water Tank Nodes:** Direct RS-485 / Modbus integration with submersible solar pump inverters for real-time kWh and flow rate telemetry.
- **Soil NPK & Moisture Probes:** Solar-powered LoRa soil telemetry stakes providing real-time nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and moisture readings.

---

## 2. Software & Ecosystem Expansions (Phase 3)
- **Dairy Cooperative Cloud Bridge:** Automated digital milk collection syncing with district dairy unions (e.g. Amul / Mother Dairy / State Milk Federations) for instant direct-to-bank farmer payments.
- **Mobile Progressive Web App (PWA):** Offline service workers with push notifications for frost warnings, heat wave advisories, and vaccination camps.
- **Micro-Credit Scoring Engine:** Exportable certified milk yield and farm production histories enabling automated credit underwriting for rural banking partners.
