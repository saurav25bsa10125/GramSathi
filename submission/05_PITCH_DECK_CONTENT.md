# 05. Hackathon Pitch Deck Content (12 Slides)

---

### Slide 1 — Title Slide
- **Title:** GramSathi (ग्रामसाथी)
- **Subtitle:** Solar-Powered Smart Village Hub & Rural Digital Operating Platform
- **Tagline:** Empowering Rural India with Decentralized IoT, Resilient Infrastructure & Digital Governance
- **Presented by:** Team GramSathi

---

### Slide 2 — The Problem
- **Erratic Water Distribution:** Unmonitored tanks overflow during power availability and run dry during peak hours.
- **Unmonitored Livestock Health:** Sub-clinical cattle illnesses cause sudden drops in milk yield and financial distress.
- **Manual Dairy Ledgers:** Paper-based milk collection leads to payment disputes and zero credit histories.
- **Agricultural Asymmetry:** Lack of personalized crop planning and complex welfare scheme discovery.

---

### Slide 3 — The Solution: GramSathi
- A **Zero-Dependency, Decentralized Digital Operating Platform** combining:
  1. Off-Grid Solar & Potable Water Automation
  2. RFID Livestock Health & Dairy Yield Ledgers
  3. ICAR-Aligned Crop Planning & e-NAM Mandi Feeds
  4. Direct Benefit Welfare Scheme Discovery
  5. Context-Aware Rural AI Assistant

---

### Slide 4 — Product Architecture & Unified Flow
- **Connected Ecosystem:** Solar Power → Water Pump → Cattle Vitals → Dairy Ledger → Crop Advisory → Mandi Rates → Welfare Schemes.
- **Hardware-Agnostic Ingestion:** Ready for LoRaWAN & ESP32 telemetry nodes.
- **Offline-First Storage:** LocalStorage repository layer with complete JSON/CSV export capabilities.

---

### Slide 5 — Core Innovation (25% Weightage)
- **Integrated Village Command Center:** Replaces isolated point apps with one synchronized village OS.
- **Transparent Agronomic Rule Engine:** "WHY THIS CROP?" reasoning shows exact soil, water, and rotation compatibility.
- **Non-Hallucinating Local Assistant:** Reads real application data directly to answer inquiries accurately.

---

### Slide 6 — Live Demonstration Highlights
- **Real-Time IoT Simulation:** Interactive scenarios for low flow, turbidity spikes, and critical battery states.
- **Animal Health Telemetry:** Multi-tab profile with body temperature, heart rate, rumination minutes, and event timelines.
- **Instant Welfare Eligibility:** 4-step wizard matching 10 verified Government of India schemes.

---

### Slide 7 — Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** Pure HTML5, CSS3 Custom Properties, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
- **Backend:** Native zero-dependency Node.js HTTP server.
- **Internationalization:** 5 Indian Languages (English, Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu).
- **Package Size:** <200 KB total footprint — instant loading on 2G/3G networks.

---

### Slide 8 — Real-World Impact (20% Weightage)
- **Water Conservation:** Prevents tank overflow and guarantees potable drinking water standard.
- **Preventive Animal Care:** Flags early fever thresholds to safeguard dairy livelihoods.
- **Digital Financial History:** Exportable milk collection records unlock formal institutional credit.

---

### Slide 9 — Feasibility & Deployment Model (15% Weightage)
- **Panchayat Wall Kiosk:** Operates in full-screen high-contrast mode on affordable displays.
- **Zero Server Setup:** Can run completely standalone in a web browser without cloud dependencies.
- **Ultra-Low Operating Cost:** Minimal hardware and power requirements.

---

### Slide 10 — Future Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Current):** Standalone Proof-of-Concept with verified datasets and simulated IoT telemetry.
- **Phase 2:** Hardware validation with ESP32 LoRaWAN collar and solar ultrasonic tank sensors.
- **Phase 3:** Integration with State Dairy Cooperatives and soil NPK telemetry probes.

---

### Slide 11 — Hackathon Evaluation Alignment
- **Innovation & Originality (25%):** Unified multi-sector village operating platform.
- **Technical Implementation (25%):** Clean zero-dependency architecture with complete persistence.
- **Real-World Impact (20%):** Addresses daily water, cattle, agriculture, and welfare challenges.
- **Feasibility & Scalability (15%):** Ultra-lightweight, offline-resilient, scalable to any Panchayat.
- **User Experience (10%):** High-contrast rural-tech UI, keyboard accessible, mobile-first responsive.
