# 04. Hackathon Demo Video Script & Walkthrough (3–5 Minutes)

**Target Duration:** 4 Minutes  
**Presenter:** Team GramSathi  

---

### Step-by-Step Demonstration Flow:

| Timestamp | Screen / Route | Action & Demonstration | Spoken Narration Script |
|---|---|---|---|
| **00:00 – 00:30** | Introduction & Problem | Show Title Screen & Village Reality | "Namaste judges. Rural villages struggle with uncoordinated off-grid solar power, erratic drinking water supply, and manual dairy records. Introducing **GramSathi**, a solar-powered smart village hub and rural digital operating platform." |
| **00:30 – 01:00** | Dashboard Command Center (`#dashboard`) | Show Village Command Center, Today's Village Brief, & Real-time Gauges | "Here on the Dashboard Command Center, village administrators see today's complete brief: overhead tank level, livestock health collar telemetry, today's milk yield, and solar PV output with battery SoC." |
| **01:00 – 01:30** | Water Simulation & Incident (`#water`) | Click **'Simulate Low Flow'** button | "Let's demonstrate real-time IoT integration. When a solar pump flow drops below threshold, the system immediately flags a low flow warning, updates the Dashboard, logs an incident in Alert Center, and prepares a simulated cellular SMS alert." |
| **01:30 – 02:00** | Livestock Hub & Dairy Records (`#livestock` & `#dairy`) | Open **Gauri (COW-01)** Profile modal; Switch tabs (Health, Milk, Timeline); Log a new milk entry | "In the Livestock Hub, each cattle has an RFID profile with collar vitals and nutrition rations. In Dairy Operations, we record morning milk collections with Fat and SNF percentages, auto-calculating daily totals and estimated earnings." |
| **02:00 – 02:30** | Agriculture & Mandi (`#agriculture` & `#mandi`) | Enter farm parameters (Rabi, Loamy, Moderate water); View **'WHY THIS CROP?'** reasoning; Click **'Check Wheat Mandi Rates'** | "Our Crop Planner evaluates ICAR agronomic rules, providing transparent reasoning for crop suitability. One click seamlessly navigates to Mandi Rates to verify real-time market prices across state terminals." |
| **02:30 – 03:00** | Government Schemes Wizard (`#schemes`) | Run 4-step Scheme Eligibility Wizard | "GramSathi simplifies welfare discovery. The 4-step wizard matches relevant Government of India direct benefit transfer schemes, such as PM-Kisan and KCC, complete with verified official portal application links." |
| **03:00 – 03:30** | AI Assistant (`#assistant`) | Click prompt chip: *'What is today's milk production and top animal?'* | "The GramSathi Rural Assistant is context-aware. Instead of hallucinating, it reads active village IoT telemetry, dairy ledgers, and crop rules directly to deliver precise answers." |
| **03:30 – 04:00** | Kiosk Display & Multi-Language (`#display` & Settings) | Launch Kiosk Wall Display mode; Switch language to Hindi (हिंदी); Show JSON backup export | "For Panchayat Bhawans, GramSathi launches a high-contrast Kiosk Wall Display mode, fully localized across 5 Indian languages. With offline storage and full JSON backups, GramSathi empowers rural digital governance." |
